    How these are calculated

    Trend indicator. Each score averages four parts scaled to -100 / +100. Mid-term: price vs 21-day EMA, 21 vs 55-day EMA, 10-day slope of the 21 EMA, 30-day return. Long-term: price vs 200-day SMA, 50 vs 200-day SMA, 30-day slope of the 200 SMA, 180-day return. Closed daily candles only.

    Funding bias. Percentile of the last 24h of Hyperliquid funding against its own daily history (60% 1-year, 40% 6-month). 50 is typical, low means shorts are paying more than usual, high means longs are. Hyperliquid's neutral rate is about +11% a year, so the percentile handles that automatically.

    OI pressure. Percentile of Binance open interest (in coins) against the last year (60%) and six months (40%). High means a lot of leverage is in the market.

    Market tension. 55% OI pressure + 30% how far funding bias is from 50 (doubled) + 15% percentile of the latest daily OI change.

    Squeeze risk. 40% crowding on that side (funding bias distance from 50) + 35% OI pressure + 25% size of the last 24h move against that side. Scores, not probabilities.

    Liquidation map. A model, not real orders. Every hourly rise in Binance OI over the last 30 days is split long/short by taker buy/sell volume, spread across 5x/10x/25x/50x/100x leverage (15/30/25/20/10%), and placed at its liquidation price. Falls in OI shrink all open levels. A level is removed once price trades through it. Positions opened before the window aren't counted.

    Binance data comes from Binance's public archive, which publishes each day's file the next day, so those numbers run up to a day behind. Hyperliquid numbers are live as of the last hourly update.
